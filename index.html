03 — OriginStarting with straws

The project started as an electric bamboo bicycle for sustainable urban mobility. To test the concept early, I built a small-scale prototype from drinking straws, the closest material I had to the way bamboo behaves structurally. It let me test proportions, geometry and stability before moving to the final material.

BeforeAfter

01 — ObjectiveThe brief

The outdoor terrace at Casa do Lago was underused and offered little environmental comfort. The goal was to reorganise it into a functional, welcoming space for the users and staff of Associação VITAE: an outdoor area that supports well-being, socialising and quiet activities in the open air.

02 — Current situationWhat was there

Today the terrace has few things to use, no shade and no clear layout. It doesn't fully meet its users' needs, is hard to adapt and has little connection to its surroundings.

03 — ProblemsWhat needed fixing

01No suitable furniture or defined seating areas→
02No shade or shelter from the weather→
03An unwelcoming space for vulnerable users→
04Underused and not very functional→
05No natural or comfortable elements→

04 — ConceptNature, calm, activity

The terrace becomes an outdoor refuge that encourages rest and time together. Warm materials, vegetation and clearly defined zones make it feel welcoming, both emotionally and physically.

A new identity for an animal reserve, made to feel family-friendly, welcoming and wild.

04
Year2025
ContextCommunication Design, IADE
TeamWith Luís Ramos
DeliverablesLogo, brand manual, look and feel, merchandise
Problem

An animal reserve needed an identity that felt family-friendly, welcoming and wild.

Approach

Keep the crocodile from the park's history, use green for sustainability and a playful illustrated campaign.

Outcome

Logo, 16-page brand manual, campaign look and feel and merchandise. Made with Luís Ramos.

Keep scrolling

01 — BriefReimagining the reserve

Monte Selvagem is an animal reserve in Portugal. The new identity had to convey a family-friendly, welcoming atmosphere. Out of respect for the park's history we kept the crocodile as the main symbol, and chose green to stand for sustainability and the idea of being "in full harmony with nature".

Wishi.

A gift finder for people who don't know what to buy, pitched in under five minutes.

MSc
Year2026
CoursePitch, Presentation and Storytelling Strategies, Politecnico di Milano
TypeTeam project
Deliverables5-minute pitch, working prototype, growth plan, TikTok videos
Problem

Secret Santa: you draw someone you barely know and get lost across Amazon, Etsy and a dozen apps.

Approach

A free gift finder that turns a few clues into ideas from several shops, paid by affiliate commissions.

Outcome

A 5-minute pitch, a working prototype, a 12-month growth plan and two TikTok videos.

wishi

Find something they've been wishi for.

Click the mascot to make it spin.

01 — Problem"You're lost."

You draw a Secret Santa name: someone you barely know. You open Amazon, then Etsy, then a few more apps. More options, still no idea what fits. In a YouGov survey, 53% of UK Christmas shoppers said it was hard to choose gifts people would like.

Wishi turns a few clues about a person into gift ideas from several shops in one place. It's free for shoppers and earns from affiliate commissions.
